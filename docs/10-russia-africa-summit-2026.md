### Venue shift
The Russia–Africa Partnership Forum communiqués, last repeated at the 2024
ministerial conference, said the third summit would be held **in Africa**. Lavrov
said on 8 Apr 2026 that it would be held in Moscow "as has been agreed on". This
is the **third summit in a row on Russian soil**, after Sochi in 2019 and
St Petersburg in 2023. African commentary has noticed this (Modern Ghana, "Once
again in Russia"; Le Pays, Chad). No official explanation for the move was found.

**Headline number:** "more than 30" of 54 states, per the Russian MFA since
20 July. It has not been updated. **No official list has been published.**
For comparison: Sochi 2019 drew all 54 states, about 43–45 of them at head-of-state
level. St Petersburg 2023 drew 49 delegations, about 17 at head-of-state level.
RT's figure of 27 for 2023 conflicts with the widely reported 17.

## 5. Points of contention and analytical notes

- **Lower turnout than past summits.** "More than 30" states by July is well
  below the 49–54 of 2019 and 2023. Russian officials' framing ("quite a
  significant achievement") prepares for a smaller count. Vice-presidential
  delegations from Uganda and Gabon suggest a lower **level** of attendance as well.
- **Recruitment of Africans for the war.** Ukrainian intelligence estimates
  more than 1,400 recruits from 39 African states. Kenya obtained a Russian
  commitment that there would be "no further enlisting". Le Pays (Chad) cites
  official protests by African governments. RAM 66 names the Alabuga Start
  drone-factory programme among summit exhibitors.
- **Promises not met.** Sochi set a target of $40 billion in trade within five
  years. Trade was $27.7 billion in 2025, up 13%. Russian FDI is under 1% of
  Africa's total. In 2023 Moscow pledged 200,000 t of free grain; about 75,000 t
  were reportedly delivered (Le Pays).
- **Sanctions exposure.** RAM 66 reports that the payments firm A7, sanctioned by
  the US on 1 Oct 2026, is among participants. This matters for African banks
  and for the planned national-currency settlements.
- **Security context.** RAM 66 notes that Air Tanzania suspended its Moscow
  flights in Sep 2026.
